# Disclaimer

**AdaptiveSurgeon is a research/engineering prototype. It is ADVISORY ONLY and
is NOT a medical device.**

- It is **not** cleared or approved by any regulatory authority (e.g. FDA, CE,
  CDSCO) and must **not** be used for clinical decision-making, diagnosis, or
  treatment.
- All data in this repository is **synthetic**. The demo videos, patients,
  procedures, outcomes, and reports are generated programmatically and do not
  represent real patients or surgeries.
- The AI components shipped here are **synthetic/heuristic** stand-ins designed
  to demonstrate the system architecture end-to-end. Their outputs (instrument
  detections, phases, skill scores, risk probabilities, advisories) are **not
  clinically validated** and carry **no clinical meaning**.
- The risk priors and copilot guidance strings are **illustrative** and do not
  reflect validated clinical evidence.
- Any future use with real, de-identified clinical data must comply with
  applicable privacy law (e.g. HIPAA/GDPR), institutional review, informed
  consent, and clinical validation + regulatory clearance before any deployment.

The system is intended to assist, never to replace, the judgment of a qualified
surgeon. Autonomous operation is explicitly out of scope.
